Nunc Dimittis — To an Engineer
at His Terminal
God, to an engineer still looking at the terminal — with restless eyes, sharpness,
but paralyzed with blurred vision. Wrinkles of age in his face. Gray hair. A
shoulder-lean that stood the test of time. Twenty-two years in service.
My child, you can now rest.
It has been 22 years. You are allowed to move your eyes off the terminal. Your
eyes now deserve some rest.
You are allowed to withdraw from the line of duty — from the call you have
been serving for 22 years.
Go, son. I release you.
Received. Recorded. Returned.
Not owned. Not authored. Given back.
Nunc Dimittis — To an Engineer at His Terminal
