Overview

Characters

possessive, destructive. Meher means grace/mercy — deliberately ironic
against her arc.
Neel Sharma — male lead. Gentle, sensitive, quiet. A working actor without
her stardom. He is the wounded observer for most of the film. Neel means
blue — poet, mourner.

Opening beat: the predator gaze
Cold open on Meher's gaze locking onto Neel from across a film set. No dialogue.
No context. The audience feels the wrongness before they know why. Three
seconds — the phenomenology of predation shown before the film asks anyone to
think. This is the first drop of the smuggled testimony.
Ending (director's decision)
The Hero's protest goes through every institution: courts dismiss the case,
ministries refuse the file, doctors close their doors, police never register the
report. Each rejection intercut with the case Neel is trying to make. He is
exhausted, alone, still moving.
Then the film cuts back to Meher.
She is still sitting on the floor of the psychiatric ward. Still rocking. Still whispering 
forgive me… forgive me… forgive me…
She does not know he tried. She never will.
Cut to black.
The protest died in the institutions. The person the protest was for continues to
suffer alone. The man who protested carries both losses. Anything softer would
betray the testimony.

Notes for prompting Veo 3.1 well
Character consistency comes from the @Meher / @Neel references, not from re-
describing them in every prompt. Keep the character references clean; add
only scene-specific attributes.
Veo 3.1 generates native audio. Every prompt above specifies the sound bed.
Trust it — do not layer external audio until you have seen what Veo produces.
16:9 is the master aspect ratio. Vertical Shorts/Reels cuts get reframed later in
an editor, not regenerated.
Prompts describe what the camera sees and hears, not narrative intent.
"Hunger. Recognition of prey." works because it directs the actor
performance; "she is falling in love with him wrongly" would not.
If a shot generates something close but not right, iterate by editing one
specific element ("more shadow across her face," "pull in slower," "no blink at
all") rather than rewriting the whole prompt.
