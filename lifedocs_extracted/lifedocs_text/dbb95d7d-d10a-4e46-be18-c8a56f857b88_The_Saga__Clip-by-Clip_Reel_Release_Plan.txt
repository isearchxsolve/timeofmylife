🎞️
The Saga — Clip-by-Clip Reel
Release Plan
🎞️A serialized reel series cut from your memoir — built to compound a
following and demo the kit, not to chase a one-day viral spike. Each clip
is a self-contained 30–60s piece with a hook, a visual, a narration
fragment, and a capture line. Post in order as a numbered series ("The
Confession, Part 1…") so viewers come back for the next.
How to run this series (read first)
Cadence: 3–4 clips/week. A series that shows up regularly beats one clip that
pops once. Consistency is the algorithm signal that actually compounds.
Platforms: YouTube Shorts is your primary (only India-viable ad-revenue lane
long-term) + Instagram Reels for reach and inbound DMs. Cross-post the
same cut to both.
The capture (non-negotiable): every clip ends with a way to convert attention
— a follow prompt, "full saga in bio," or a soft "made with my own AI film kit →
DM me." A view you don't capture is heat that fades by tomorrow.
Double duty: these are also your kit demo. "An AI kit that can render a human
story this well" sells the kit better than any feature list. Watermark subtly or
note it in the caption.
Privacy line: this plan uses the universal/philosophical beats (the war, the
truce, the eagle, insomnia, plastic hearts). It deliberately leaves out the private
Julie confession. Keep it that way unless you decide otherwise — the universal
beats are both safer and more shareable.
