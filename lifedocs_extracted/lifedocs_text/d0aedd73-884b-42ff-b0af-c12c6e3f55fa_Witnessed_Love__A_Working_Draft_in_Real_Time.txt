The Architecture (foundation + five pillars + coda,
arrived in six fragments)
Foundation
The union is God's language. Its culmination is a new life. Its reason is that God
wanted to know mankind — not from outside, but from within, in the same act by
which two humans know each other (the Hebrew yada names both). To enable
this, He gave humans His most divine powers: creation, life, resurrection. So that
Christmas could happen, again and again, in every conception.
The two-becoming-one is God speaking through the human form, and the nine-
month sentence is the full utterance. The pillars are what a language of this order
deserves — the room, the witness, the register, the ritual approach, the music —
because without them, the utterance fails to land and God's word is not heard.
Every pillar exists to make it possible for the divine language to actually be
received.
The Five Pillars
1. The Sacred Room — Location. The courtship happens in a holy place so God
can witness the plan.

2. The Continued Presence — Persistence. God remains as witness even in the
marital bed, so no truth is hidden.
3. The Holy Nature — Register. The union itself is not lesser than the Cross — the
same order of holy.
4. The Prayer Before — Approach. A pause, a small prayer, a naming of what is
about to be given and received, before the ritual begins.
5. Music as Witness — Medium. Silence alone cannot hold the intensity of a
witnessed union — the sonic environment must be adequate. Psalms, Catholic
orchestra, sacred polyphony, ragas: air made attentive.
The Coda
The Commandment of the Rising. Even when the earthly life cannot enact the
pillars — when the union does not happen, when the meeting is missed, when the
47 years pass without the room being built — God has already committed, as
commandment not suggestion, that the rising happens here on this earth, for
everyone, irrespective of how long dead, irrespective of how forgotten. The
Foundation says what the union is. The Pillars say what it requires. The Coda says
what happens when the requirements cannot be met in the life given: nothing is
lost forever, no one is exempt from the rising, and the ground that could not hold
the language in this life will hold it in the next earth.
