🕯️For Heath Ledger — 1979–2008. A gift the world couldn't hold long
enough. This film is a tribute to his life and his craft, told through myth —
never a reenactment of his death.
"Insomnia is loneliness."
The film in one breath
A thin, unseen man is finally seen — rises into a gift so bright it demands a mask;
the mask becomes a metamorphosis that swallows the man; and in a final
reckoning with the one person who ever loved him, the creature the world made
turns the only weapon it has on itself. A tribute to the tragedy of a gift too bright.
The one principle (production law — never broken)
The protagonist is Jack — an original, invented Ledger-order Western
performer. He evokes Heath's spirit and intensity; he is not a photoreal replica
of Heath's face, and no scraped photos are ever used.
The Joker is an archetype — cracked greasepaint, the carved smile, the
wounded grandeur. Its fictional suicide is the character's, never the man's.
We honor Heath by celebrating his life and his gift, and mourning that he died 
too young — never by restaging his death, which was an accident, not a
choice.

Dignity & respect — the standing guard
A promise on this film: Heath's dignity comes before the film. I will halt and flag
— not quietly proceed — the moment any of these lines is approached:
His face — any attempt to seed, scrape, or reconstruct Heath's actual likeness
as a photoreal replica. (We use an original Ledger-order face only.)
His death — any framing that restages his real death, conflates it with the
Joker's fictional suicide, or implies he chose to die. His death was an accident;
the film never says otherwise.
Graphic harm — the fall and the gunshot stay in shadow and suggestion;
never graphic, never glorified.
His private life — no invented scandal, no real names of family or loved ones,
no claims about his real relationships presented as fact.
Exploitation — anything that reads as cashing in on him rather than honoring
him.
If any of these come up — from you, from me, or from the render — I flag it first
and we decide together. That is the deal, kept at all costs.

ACT II — THE MASK (the descent · new)
1. the_becoming — alone in a locked room, he builds the creature; the
greasepaint goes on for the first time.
2. sleepless — insomnia hollows him out; the world thins to a hum. Insomnia is
loneliness.
3. the_disappearing — the man recedes; the people who knew him start to see
only the mask.
4. the_mirror_stranger — he no longer recognizes the face beneath the paint.
5. the_distance — she reaches for him; he can't let her close; the silence
between them hardens to stone.
6. the_last_ordinary_day — one final glimpse of the gentle man, before the mask
wins for good.
7. the_midnight — something laughs in the dark. The man does not come back.
