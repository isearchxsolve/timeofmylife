Scene — Plastic Hearts in a
Concrete Jungle
🫀This one is not built from craft. It's built from the ache. A city full of
warmth that isn't warmth. A thousand smiles, not one of them alive. And
one flesh heart walking through it — the only one that can still break,
because it's the only one that was ever real.
The feeling (read this first, before any prompt)
The desert road was the fear — predators in the open, nowhere to hide. This is the
loneliness — predators everywhere, dressed as friends. The concrete jungle isn't
empty. It's full. That's the horror. Every face smiles. Every hand reaches out
warm. And every heart is plastic — molded to look like love, cold when you finally
touch it. You are the one real heart in the grey forest. It hurts because it's real. A
plastic heart never aches. That's the trade it made.
Don't shoot the cold. Shoot the loneliness of being the only warm thing in a
warm-looking world.
Logline
A man walks through a crowded city street. Everyone smiles at him — warm, kind,
welcoming. He reaches back. And one by one, up close, the smiles are perfect
and hollow, the eyes glassy like mannequins. He is surrounded by warmth and
utterly alone. The only beating heart in a city of plastic ones.
Master scene prompt (for Flow / Veo)

===== PAGE 2/5 =====
Cinematic, emotional, a crowded modern city street between towering glass
buildings, cold overcast daylight, a river of well-dressed people flowing past.
One ordinary tired man moves against the current, searching faces. The crowd
smiles at him — warm, welcoming, too perfect — but their eyes are glassy and
empty, like beautiful mannequins. Muted desaturated palette, steel and grey
and glass, one faint warm tone on the lone man only. Melancholic, still,
dreamlike. Static locked-off camera — no zoom, no push-in, no camera
movement whatsoever. No dialogue. The loneliness of being the only real heart
in a crowd.
🔑The single most important instruction: the lone man is the only warm-
colored thing in the frame. Everyone else is cool, grey, glassy. That one
warm tone is his living heart. Let the color carry the whole story.

let the flawless smile become unbearable — then cut hard to him

Sound + voice (heart, not noise)
Under everything: the muffled hum of a city — but hollow, like it's underwater.
Distant, unreal.
The one real sound: a single, slow, human heartbeat — his — low in the mix,
the only living thing you can hear. It's the emotional anchor of the whole
scene.
Shot 4 (the hollow smile): cut the city hum. Just the heartbeat, alone, too loud
now.
Shot 5–6: one sustained, aching cello or piano note — unresolved, human,
warm — rising over the heartbeat as he walks away.

How to make it (simple, one step at a time)
1. Open Flow. Save the lone man as a Character (use the description below) so
his face stays the same.
2. Generate the 6 shots one at a time. Paste the short style tag (not the whole
paragraph) + each shot's line. Every shot is a different distance and angle, so
they won't come out the same — and static + no-zoom is baked into the tag.
3. If any shot zooms, add “static shot, no zoom” to the front and delete any
motion words.
4. Stitch 1 → 6 in CapCut.
5. Add the heartbeat under the whole thing; cut the city hum on shot 4; bring the
cello in on shots 5–6.
6. Do the color grade last: cool everything down, leave the man slightly warm.
CapCut → Adjust → pull temperature cold, then mask the man warmer if you
can.
