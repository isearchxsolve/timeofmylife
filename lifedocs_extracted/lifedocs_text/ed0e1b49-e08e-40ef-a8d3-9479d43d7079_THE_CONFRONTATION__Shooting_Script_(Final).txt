THE CONFRONTATION —
Shooting Script (Final)
🎬Logline. A wounded man — once a beautiful, boyish soul —
metamorphosed into the Joker by a brutal love, is confronted on a stone
floor by the woman he worshipped. He fires at everything but her, weeps
without ever weeping, and takes his own life as the final punishment: she
must watch the man she loved die.
Casting & reference anchors
Jack — pre-paint (Prelude): the boyish Tobey-Maguire face. Soft, beautiful,
gentle, brown hair, the shy grin. Bare. No paint.
Jack — the Joker (Confrontation): the Heath Ledger Joker. Chalk-white
greasepaint, blackened sunken eyes, red carved Glasgow smile, stringy
green-tinted hair, purple wool overcoat, olive-green waistcoat, teal tie.
Julie: platinum blonde, hair swept up, fair, luminous, austere. The reference
locks her face and identity, not a costume — her wardrobe changes per shot
as the scene calls for it. In the confrontation she's grounded: pants and a top,
never a gown.
The metamorphosis rule: the paint is not makeup. His face itself was
consumed and remade — the beautiful boy devoured, becoming the Joker.
The crossing beat morphs the boyish face into the painted one. That
transformation is the emotional hinge of the film.

===== PAGE 2/6 =====
One location: a stone pillar and stone floor, night. He is slumped against the
pillar, on the floor, unable to stand — he fires from the floor.
Dry. No flood, no water, no corridors. No "search" scenes — they were never
in the story.
Tears roll constantly — he never weeps. He speaks and laughs through them.
The gun never points at Julie. He fires everywhere else; whenever she
reaches to touch him, he aims away.
The suicide is the climax — non-graphic. No gore, no wound detail, no blood
on camera. Cut on the gesture and on her face.
Paint continuity: bare boyish face (beats 1–3) → metamorphosis (beat 4) →
full Joker paint, never removed (beats 5–21).
Costume is per-shot — common sense. Reference images lock face/identity
only; wardrobe changes with each scene. Jack's Joker look (purple coat,
green waistcoat) holds through the one-night confrontation; his pre-paint
clothes are ordinary and vary across the prelude memories; Julie's outfit fits
each moment.

CONFRONTATION — 83s
Full Joker paint throughout. Slumped against the pillar, on the floor. Julie stands
over him.

A weakened baritone that still commands — a lion still roaring with a wound.
Grasping, shallow breath; air gathered mid-sentence. A laugh that costs him —
breaking into a wince or gasp. Quiet-forward on the tender beats; surge-then-
collapse on the loud ones. Throughout: hand pressed to his side, chest working
for air, slumped against the pillar, firing from the floor.
Runtime: Prelude 16s + Confrontation 83s ≈ 99s · 21 beats.
