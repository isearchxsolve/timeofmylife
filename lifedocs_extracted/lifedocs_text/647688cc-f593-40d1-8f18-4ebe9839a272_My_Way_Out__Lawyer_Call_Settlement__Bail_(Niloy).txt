🗣️ What to say (read this out — it's calm and
complete)
"My name is Niloy Das. I have a loan I cannot repay — around ₹90 lakhs. I have
lost my job and have no assets or income. The lenders have indicated they want
to settle. There is a warrant against me, and it is only because of this loan — I
have not committed any violence or any crime. I want to (1) protect myself from
arrest through anticipatory bail, and (2) decide whether to settle or file for
insolvency, and handle it properly."
❓ The questions to ask the lawyer
1. Should I go for a negotiated settlement or formal insolvency — which is
better given I have no job and no assets?
2. Can you file for anticipatory bail so I'm protected from arrest while this is
being sorted?
