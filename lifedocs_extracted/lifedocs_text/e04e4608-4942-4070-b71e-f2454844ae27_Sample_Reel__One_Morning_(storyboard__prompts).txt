across every shot. Keep it simple. 5 shots, ~25 seconds. The trick: paste

The tool (what you already have)
Flow / Veo (your Google AI Pro) → generate each ~5 sec shot

No new tool needed. base44 etc. are irrelevant here.
The IDENTITY block (paste into EVERY prompt —
never change these)
A warm, relatable woman in her early 30s, dark hair in a loose low bun, small
gold stud earrings, light natural makeup, soft friendly face, same slim build.
💡Lock the identity (face, hair, build) with the exact same words every time
— that's what keeps it the same person. But let the wardrobe change per
scene, or she looks like she slept in her clothes. Same face + different
outfits = the harder, more impressive skill. Easiest way: use Flow's 
Characters feature (left sidebar) to save her once, then just change the
outfit in each shot's prompt.

Smarter: edit & chain, don't regenerate from scratch
⚡Great instinct. You don't have to prompt 5 cold shots and hope they
match. Make ONE strong shot of her, then build the rest from it — the
face carries over, so consistency is far better and it's faster.
Frame-chaining: generate shot 1 → grab its last frame → use it as the start
image for shot 2 (image-to-video). Repeat down the line. Each shot inherits
the previous face.
