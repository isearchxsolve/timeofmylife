🎞️Your first job after the current run finishes. A tiny, self-contained proof film built
from the anchors you already have — 3–4 shots, ~30 minutes of render. This
becomes the clip that sells the kit and headlines every post. No new film. Just this.
Logline
At the end of a long road, a weary man of forty-seven meets the angel who has walked every
mile beside him — and finds his truce. A wordless 30-second memoir, distilled from The Saga
of the Beholder (Chapter V — The Beholder's Truce).
Why this works as a demo
It's short enough to actually finish — 3–4 shots, one render session.
It proves the hard stuff in 30 seconds: a consistent human face, a second consistent
character, a real film grade, and an emotional hold (not just a moving filter).
It's emotional, not technical — buyers feel it before they analyze it. That feeling is what
sells.
It uses only what you already have:
man_anchor.png (the man) and woman_anchor.png (the
angel), current engine, current grade.

Grade & sound
Grade: your current look — Kodachrome warmth, halation on the highlights, fine film grain,
slightly desaturated shadows. Don't change the pipeline; the demo should look exactly like
what buyers will get.
Sound: silence + wind, and a single held piano note under shots 3–4. Let the image
breathe.
Title card: THE BEHOLDER'S TRUCE — then small: rendered entirely on a free GPU.
Render budget
Core film = 4 shots × ~8–9 min ≈ 32 min. That's your ~30-minute target.
Shot 5 is optional (+~8 min) if quota allows.
