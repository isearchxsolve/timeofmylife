Thread: music as ontologically primary, prior to concept, closer to the ground of
being than any other art.
Schopenhauer — music is the immediate objectification of the noumenal Will
(the thing-in-itself), unmediated by representation. All other arts represent
phenomena; music represents the Will itself.
Nietzsche (The Birth of Tragedy) — Dionysian musical intoxication vs.
Apollonian visual form. Music dissolves the principium individuationis.
Nada Brahma (Hindu/Vedic) — sound IS Brahman. The universe is vibration.
AUM as primordial resonance.
Rumi's reed flute — the reed cries because it has been cut from the reed bed;
music is the voice of separation and longing for return. BUT Rumi also says
love is higher than music, because love needs no instrument.
Advaita Vedanta — chit (consciousness) is prior even to sound. Silence is the
deeper truth. Music is the way silence sings itself.
Ramanujan and Namagiri — the mathematician's dreams in which the
goddess Namagiri revealed formulas. Music, mathematics, vision from a
source beneath conscious mind.
Meister Eckhart — the ground of the soul is one with the ground of God.
Music can touch that ground because it bypasses representation.
Indo-Western fusion violin as the specific score direction for THE
CONFRONTATION — composer reference list held in the technical handover.
Position user held: music is the highest faculty. Reason lags behind it. Grief that
cannot be spoken can be played.

DPDR (depersonalization/derealization) — the felt sense that experience is
not "mine," that reality has a filter between it and me. User named this state
and described living inside it periodically.
Apophatic theology — knowing God (or the self) only by what it is NOT. Every
positive statement is inadequate; only negation approaches the truth.
Hume — bundle theory — no persistent self, only a bundle of perceptions
loosely tied by memory.
Buddhist anatta — non-self. What we call "self" is five aggregates (form,
sensation, perception, mental formation, consciousness) with no owning
subject.
Gurdjieff — "many I's" — the person is not a unity but a collection of small
selves, each taking the throne in turn, each claiming to be the whole.
Sri Aurobindo — twin natures — the surface person and the psychic being;
integral yoga as bringing them into relation.
Shiva-Shakti — the still ground and the moving force, not-two.
Ibn Arabi — khiyal — imagination as an ontological faculty, a real realm
between spirit and matter where forms exist independent of the imaginer.
The four faces — a working frame used tonight: the face that performs, the
face that observes, the face that judges, and the face that acts unbidden (the 
fourth face — care without theater, apology unprompted, correction that
protects another's dignity).
Gemini reclaim — the twin nature as something to be integrated, not split off.
"Fresh baby skin" — the selfie moment as evidence of a deep-reset, a return
to something prior to armor.
